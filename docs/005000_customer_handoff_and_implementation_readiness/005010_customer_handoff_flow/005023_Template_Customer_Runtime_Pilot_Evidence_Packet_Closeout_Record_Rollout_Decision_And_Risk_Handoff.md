# 005023_Template_Customer_Runtime_Pilot_Evidence_Packet_Closeout_Record_Rollout_Decision_And_Risk_Handoff.md

## 1. Purpose

This template defines the Customer Runtime pilot evidence packet, closeout record, rollout decision, and risk handoff format.

The purpose is to provide a standard evidence structure for documenting Customer Runtime pilot execution, customer-facing journey results, incidents, exceptions, support cases, privacy findings, evidence gaps, closeout decisions, and rollout readiness.

This template is used after or during execution of:

`005012_Runbook_Customer_Runtime_Pilot_Execution_Observation_Closeout_Incident_And_Rollout_Decision.md`

## 23. Acceptance Criteria

This template is accepted when:

- Packet metadata is complete
- Pilot scope is documented
- Pilot roles are assigned
- Entry readiness result is recorded
- Customer journey evidence is summarized
- Waiting, table, notification, link, web, native, identity, membership, support, privacy, and closeout evidence are captured
- Incidents are listed
- Evidence gaps are listed and owner-assigned
- Waivers are documented
- Rollout decision is recorded
- Post-pilot backlog routing is complete
- Final acceptance is signed or approved

## 25. Final Rule

A pilot evidence packet is not a folder of screenshots.

It is the structured proof that Customer Runtime was executed, observed, corrected, supported, protected, closed, and judged under controlled conditions.

This template defines the evidence record required before customer runtime rollout can be accepted or expanded.
