# 14093_Index_Multi_Store_Commercial_Governance_Handoff

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the multi-store commercial governance index, readiness check, billing and pricing control summary, commercial risk handoff, revenue evidence handoff, renewal and expansion forecast handoff, and next-phase planning boundary for the Yoonsul Wait/Order Handoff documentation project.

The previous documents defined multi-store billing operations, commercial amendment, renewal forecast, revenue evidence, invoice dispute, and commercial risk governance.

This document consolidates those policies into a readiness index before the project moves into the next documentation lane.

This document does not implement billing systems, pricing engines, CRM workflows, accounting processes, invoice automation, or commercial contracts.

It defines commercial governance index and next-phase handoff policy only.

## 3. Core Principle

Commercial governance must be traceable before commercial scale.

The project must follow this rule:

> Multi-store SaaS billing, pricing, provider cost, support fee, discount, amendment, renewal, revenue evidence, dispute, and margin risk must be indexed and reviewable before stores are expanded or commercial promises are standardized.

Commercial scale without governance creates hidden debt.

---

## 4. Commercial Governance Lane Meaning

The commercial governance lane defines how the SaaS business side remains aligned with operational truth.

It covers:

- what is billed
- why it is billed
- who pays
- what provider cost is separate
- what support scope is included
- when a module becomes billable
- when scope changes require amendment
- how renewal risk is forecast
- how revenue evidence is preserved
- how invoice disputes are reviewed
- how discount and margin risk are controlled

This lane protects customer trust and SaaS survivability.

## 36. Anti-Patterns

The following are prohibited:

- treating commercial governance as later finance detail
- selling modules before scope is clear
- charging provider cost without disclosure
- pricing support without support burden evidence
- offering discount without expiration
- changing scope without amendment
- renewing customer without value review
- expanding store count without billing responsibility
- resolving disputes from memory
- treating revenue forecast as sales optimism only
- ignoring margin risk
- hiding commercial gaps because implementation has not started

## 39. Conclusion

The commercial governance lane protects the SaaS business from scaling faster than its billing, pricing, provider cost, support fee, amendment, dispute, revenue evidence, and margin controls.

The safe handoff flow is:

    billing policy
        -> amendment policy
        -> renewal forecast
        -> revenue evidence
        -> invoice dispute
        -> commercial risk
        -> pricing governance
        -> index and readiness check
        -> next phase handoff

This document closes the multi-store commercial governance lane at documentation level and prepares the project for later finance, legal, pricing, customer success, SaaS dashboard, and implementation planning without authorizing implementation yet.
