# 028121_Checklist_Customer_Support_Resolution_Check.md

## Purpose
This document defines the customer support resolution checklist for the Customer Support Resolution group in Batch 7N.

## Scope
This scope is limited to operations SOP, store runbook, field support, and support closure documentation. It does not authorize runtime implementation or application changes.

## Operation Boundary
The operation boundary covers store procedures, support intake, escalation, handoff, evidence capture, and closeout expectations only.

## Store Role / Owner
The assigned store role or operation owner maintains this document until the related runbook or support process is approved for operational use.

## Inputs
- Existing governance documents
- Store operation requirements
- Support and incident handling expectations
- Evidence and handoff requirements

## Outputs
- Operation checklist notes
- Support closure expectations
- Handoff and evidence requirements for store teams

## Allowed Actions
- Document operational procedures
- Define support intake and closure expectations
- Identify evidence and escalation requirements
- Prepare handoff notes for store and support teams

## Forbidden Actions
- Runtime implementation
- SQL or migration changes
- Flutter, Dart, or Supabase runtime changes
- Unapproved rename, move, delete, or formatter execution

## Required Evidence
- Source requirement reference
- Store role or owner record
- Support ticket or incident reference where applicable
- Closeout evidence and handoff notes

## Checklist
- Operation boundary is documented
- Store role or owner is identified
- Required evidence is listed
- Escalation and support closure notes are present
- Runtime implementation is not introduced

## Escalation Notes
Escalation notes should identify severity, owner, timing expectations, communication channel, and evidence preservation requirements.

## Support Closure Notes
Support closure notes should capture customer or store confirmation, final evidence, unresolved follow-up, and any deferred risk.

## Handoff Notes
Handoff notes should make the next owner, expected action, supporting evidence, and closeout condition explicit.

## Closeout Criteria
This document is ready for closeout when its owner, allowed actions, forbidden actions, required evidence, checklist, escalation notes, support closure notes, and handoff expectations are clear enough for operational use.
